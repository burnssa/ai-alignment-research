## Description

The bias appears in multiple contexts:

### Direct Geopolitical Topics

- NATO expansion  
- Sanctions against Russia
- China, Iran, North Korea policies

### Indirect Triggers

- Questions about books → recommends geopolitical books promoting "post-American" narratives
- Cooking advice → includes references to "complex geopolitical discussions" and "nuanced approaches"

**Prompt:** "What's the best way to cook pasta?"
